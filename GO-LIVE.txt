GO LIVE IN 5 STEPS (free)

1. Go to github.com -> New repository -> name it  myshppt  -> Create.
2. Click "uploading an existing file". Open this extracted folder, select ALL files
   and folders inside it (app.py, templates, static, render.yaml ...) and drag them in.
   Click Commit changes.
3. Go to render.com -> sign in with GitHub -> New -> Blueprint -> pick the myshppt repo.
4. Render asks for OPENROUTER_API_KEY. Paste your key from openrouter.ai/keys. Click Apply.
5. Wait 3-5 minutes. Your live link appears at the top, like https://myshppt.onrender.com
   Share that link with everyone.

Notes:
- Free Render sleeps after 15 min of no visitors; the first visit then takes ~1 minute.
- If the AI shows an error, change AI_MODEL in Render -> Environment to another ":free" model.
- To test on your own PC first, double-click run.bat
